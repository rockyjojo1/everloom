## Review decisions

- Sections 11, 12, 14, 16 and 18 are approved visual direction.
- Section 13 is approved animation direction and contact-pose language, not final rigging or timing data.
- Section 17 is approved relative-scale direction. Printed measurements remain provisional until reconciled with Everloom world units, collisions and camera readability.
- Sections 19 and 20 are approved supplementary technical direction. They extend rather than replace sections 02, 03, 05 and 06.
- Section 15 is preserved as inspiration but requires an original Everloom redesign before production. Its current composition is too close to RuneScape and contains a RuneScape-specific Wiki control. Preserve compact information density and usability, but do not reproduce copyrighted layout or presentation elements.

## Implementation boundary

These PNGs are references, not runtime assets. Models, textures, individual icons, particle systems, rigs and animations must be derived, integrated, performance-tested and visually reviewed in the live game.
