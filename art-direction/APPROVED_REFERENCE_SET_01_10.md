## Rules for future prompts and implementation

- Name the exact approved sheet and manifest IDs for every visual task.
- Preserve the low-poly geometry, compact OSRS-like readability, muted natural palette, strong silhouettes, Loom motifs, elevated three-quarter presentation, and restrained modern lighting shown in these sheets.
- Do not replace approved designs with pixel art, voxel art, generic primitives, glossy mobile-game styling, or invented Haiku art direction.
- Treat sheets as production references, not runtime-ready assets. Runtime code must ultimately point to derived, reviewed game assets such as GLB models, textures, icons, VFX, and animation data.
- Sections 02, 03, and 05 still need later technical supplements for broader character silhouette variation, secondary NPC roles, and equipment grip/origin/scale/contact specifications.
- Do not claim an asset visually complete until it is implemented in the game and verified in gameplay screenshots.
